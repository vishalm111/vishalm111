### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **Aspiring Data Scientist & Machine Learning Practitioner** currently pursuing my **M.Sc. in Data Science at REVA University, Bengaluru (2025–2027)**, holding a **B.Sc. in Computer Science & Statistics from St. Joseph's University, Bengaluru (2022–2025)**.
